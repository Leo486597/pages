Enforcement cases执法案例

Breaking the US chip-export rules is a crime: up to about $1M in fines and 20 years in prison per violation, under the Export Control Reform Act (50 U.S.C. § 4819). Since 2025 there have been arrests, indictments and large seizures — but no prison sentence has landed in any AI-chip export case yet.违反美国芯片出口规则是犯罪：依据《出口管制改革法》（50 U.S.C. § 4819），每项违规最高可处约 100 万美元罚款和 20 年监禁。自 2025 年以来已有逮捕、起诉和大额查扣——但尚无任何 AI 芯片出口案件判处监禁。

What has actually landed实际已发生的

1

Each unlicensed export is a crime under ECRA (50 U.S.C. § 4819): up to 20 years in prison and a fine up to $1M, or up to twice the gain for a company. Smuggling counts add up to 10 years; money laundering up to 20.依据 ECRA（50 U.S.C. § 4819），每宗无证出口均属犯罪：最高 20 年监禁和 100 万美元罚款，公司则最高按所得利润两倍计罚。走私罪名另加最高 10 年，洗钱最高 20 年。

DOJ · 8 Dec 2025

2

Seven US criminal matters have reached arrest or indictment since August 2025, alongside a fraud case in Singapore and a forgery case in Taiwan.自 2025 年 8 月以来，已有七宗美国刑事案件进入逮捕或起诉阶段，另有新加坡的一宗欺诈案和台湾的一宗伪造文书案。

DOJ · 19 Nov 2025

3

The money hits before any ruling. Operation Gatekeeper seized over $50M in GPUs and cash; another case seeks forfeiture of 50 H200 GPUs; Singapore seized a S$38M house and froze accounts.在任何判决之前，钱已先被动。“守门人行动”查扣逾 5000 万美元的 GPU 及现金；另一宗案件寻求没收 50 块 H200 GPU；新加坡查扣一栋价值 3800 万新元的洋房并冻结账户。

DOJ · 8 Dec 2025 · Tom's Hardware · Jul 2026 press媒体

What the pattern shows案件显露的规律

Seizures — and a roughly 33% stock drop for Super Micro, which was not charged — have hit before any ruling. Intermediary companies are rarely charged; their staff are. BIS listings fall on Chinese buyers, not on the people doing the diverting. Outside the US, Singapore and Taiwan reached for fraud and forgery law, not export-control law.查扣——以及未被起诉的美超微约 33% 的股价下跌——都发生在任何判决之前。中间商公司很少被起诉，被起诉的是其员工。BIS 清单针对的是中国买家，而非实施转运的人。在美国以外，新加坡和台湾援引的是欺诈和伪造文书法，而非出口管制法。
