Legal supply合法供给

What is approved and what actually arrives are very different. Even the licensed chips reach buyers in a trickle, through two gates: Washington's export license and Beijing's import approval.获批的数量与真正到货的数量相差悬殊。即便是已获许可的芯片，也只能涓滴到货，须同时通过两道关：华盛顿的出口许可，以及北京的进口审批。

Domestic chips国产芯片

The gap缺口
