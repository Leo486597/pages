Four tests decide the US gate美国关口的四项判定

Test 1: Is it 3A090?判定一：是否属于 3A090？

TPP ≥ 4,800, or ≥ 1,600 with high density. If yes, a license is needed for China, HK, Macau and China-HQ buyers anywhere.

TPP ≥ 4,800，或高密度条件下 ≥ 1,600。若是，则出口至中国内地、香港、澳门及任何地方的中国总部买家均需许可证。

Test 2: Under the H200 line?判定二：是否低于 H200 线？

TPP < 21,000 and bandwidth < 6,500 GB/s: case-by-case逐案审查. Above: presumption of denial推定拒绝.

TPP < 21,000 且带宽 < 6,500 GB/s：逐案审查case-by-case。超过：推定拒绝presumption of denial。

Test 3: Named in a letter?判定三：是否被告知函点名？

The April 2025 is-informed letters caught the H20 and MI308, below the thresholds but licensed anyway.

2025 年 4 月的告知函点名 H20 和 MI308：虽低于门槛，仍须许可证。

Test 4: Below all of that?判定四：以上均不满足？

No US license for normal buyers. Entity List实体清单, military end use and diversion rules still apply.

普通买家无需美国许可证。但实体清单Entity List、军事最终用途及转移规则仍然适用。
