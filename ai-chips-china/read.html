The story in seven chapters七章读懂来龙去脉

Since October 2022, Washington has treated the best AI chips as a national-security item and kept them out of China with a license system that tightened every few months. Beijing answered with a second gate of its own, unpublished but real, that steers buyers to domestic chips.

The result today: only a US license makes it lawful to put a controlled chip in a Chinese buyer's hands, and that route works in practice only for Nvidia and AMD. What stays open is chips under the license line, such as the RTX PRO 5500, and compute rented from a data center outside China that no Chinese company owns. Demand is real, about 1.6 million chips short in 2026 by one estimate (cnyes citing Bloomberg Intelligence, 9 Jul 2026) estimated, and so is enforcement.

自 2022 年 10 月起，华盛顿将最先进的 AI 芯片视为国家安全物项，以许可证制度阻止其进入中国，且每隔数月便收紧一次。北京则以自己的“第二道关”回应：未公开发布，却切实存在，引导买家转向国产芯片。

结果是：如今只有美国许可证才能让受管制芯片合法交到中国买家手中，而这条路实际上只有英伟达和 AMD 走得通。仍然开放的，是低于许可线的芯片（如 RTX PRO 5500），以及从中国境外、非中资数据中心租用的算力。需求真实存在，据一项估算（钜亨网引述彭博行业研究，2026 年 7 月 9 日）估算，2026 年缺口约 160 万片；执法同样真实。
