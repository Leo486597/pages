Can high-end AI chips be sold into China? 高端 AI 芯片还能卖进中国吗？

Some can, through two gates. Washington must license the chip, or the chip must sit below its thresholds. Beijing must then let it in, and it steers buyers toward domestic chips.

部分可以，但要过两道关。华盛顿一关：芯片须获得美国许可证，或性能低于管制门槛。北京一关：还须获准进口，而北京正引导买家转向国产芯片。

Today the H200 can be licensed, from the US only, with a 25% tariff. Blackwell (B200, B300) stays shut. The real volume is in cards just under the line, such as the RTX PRO 5500.

目前 H200 可申请许可证，但只能从美国发货，并须缴纳 25% 关税。Blackwell（B200、B300）仍被禁止。真正的出货量在刚好低于门槛的显卡上，例如 RTX PRO 5500。

Two gates, both must open两道关，缺一不可

A chip reaches a Chinese buyer only when both governments say yes. Nvidia says so in its own filing.

芯片要到达中国买家手中，两国政府都必须点头。英伟达在其财报文件中亦如此表述。

Chip maker芯片厂商

Nvidia, AMD, Intel. Applies for the license; in practice only the maker can, because it alone holds the US shipment data the rule asks for.

英伟达、AMD、英特尔。由厂商申请许可证；实际上只有厂商能申请，因为规则要求的美国出货数据只有它掌握。

What a newcomer needs to know入门须知

Six facts, each one click from its source.

六条要点，每条一键直达来源。

1

The test is a number. A chip is controlled when its Total Processing Performance (TPP, compute × bit length) reaches 4,800, or 1,600 with high density.

管不管，看数字。芯片的总处理性能（TPP，即算力 × 位宽）达到 4,800，或在高密度条件下达到 1,600，即受管制。

88 FR 73458 · 25 Oct 2023

2

The H200 line. Since 15 Jan 2026, chips under TPP 21,000 and 6,500 GB/s memory bandwidth get case-by-case review, if shipped from the US.

H200 线。自 2026 年 1 月 15 日起，TPP 低于 21,000 且显存带宽低于 6,500 GB/s 的芯片，从美国出口时可获逐案审查。

91 FR 1684 · 15 Jan 2026

3

Where the company is based counts, not only where the chip goes. A China-headquartered firm needs a license in every country.

看总部所在地，不只看芯片去向。总部位于中国的企业，在任何国家采购都需要许可证。

BIS guidance · 31 May 2026

4

Demand far exceeds legal supply. ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent ordered over 3M RTX PRO 5500 cards; supply covers 5 to 10%.

需求远超合法供给。字节跳动、阿里巴巴和腾讯订购了逾 300 万张 RTX PRO 5500，供给仅能满足 5% 至 10%。

TrendForce · 21 Oct 2026 press媒体

5

Renting compute abroad is lawful today, from a data center that is not Chinese-owned. It is the most watched route, and a pending bill would close it.

在境外租用算力目前合法，前提是数据中心并非中资所有。这是受审查最严的路径，一项待审法案可能将其关闭。

H.R. 2683 · passed House 12 Jan 20262026 年 1 月 12 日众议院通过

6

Breaking the rules is a crime. Up to $1M and 20 years per violation. Smuggling cases since 2025 total billions of dollars in servers.

违规即犯罪。每项违规最高罚款 100 万美元、监禁 20 年。2025 年以来的走私案件涉及数十亿美元的服务器。

50 U.S.C. 4819 · checked 31 Oct 2026
