Jurisdictions司法辖区

Where a chip can go is not just about its destination. The US rule follows the buyer's headquarters: a company headquartered in mainland China needs a US license to buy a controlled chip in any country — Singapore, the UAE, anywhere. A chip can also be caught a second time when it is re-exported or transshipped through a third place. So four places shape this trade. In three of them — Hong Kong, Singapore, the UAE — local law does not itself ban these chips; the control that reaches them is US law.

一枚芯片能去哪里，不只取决于运往何处。美国规则看的是买家的总部：总部在中国大陆的企业，在任何国家采购受管制芯片都需要美国许可证——新加坡、阿联酋，概莫能外。芯片经第三地再出口或转运时，还可能被二次管住。因此有四个地方塑造着这场贸易。其中三地——香港、新加坡、阿联酋——当地法律本身并不禁止这些芯片；真正管住它们的是美国法律。

Mainland China中国大陆

A controlled chip meets a US license on one side and Beijing's own, unpublished approval on the other.

受管制芯片一边遇到美国许可证，另一边遇到北京自己的、未公开的审批。

Under US law. Mainland China is Country Group D:5. A license is required for 3A090 chips; H200-class chips get case-by-case review only when shipped from the US. Re-exports from third countries and transfers inside China stay under a presumption of denial推定拒绝 — so a controlled chip already in China cannot lawfully be resold there without a license.

按美国法律。中国大陆属于 D:5 国家组。3A090 芯片须持许可证；H200 级芯片仅在自美国发货时可获逐案审查。自第三国再出口及在中国境内转移，均维持推定拒绝presumption of denial——因此已在中国境内的受管制芯片，未经许可不得在当地合法转售。

91 FR 1684 · 15 Jan 2026 · NVDA 10-Q · 26 Aug 2026

Local law. China's own Export Control Law governs the country's exports — compute, models or technology flowing out — not GPU imports, so it rarely touches an inbound chip. What does bite is the Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law and the blocking rules: a firm in China that refuses Chinese customers in order to obey US rules can face claims — a direct conflict for any business that serves China while following US law. The Cybersecurity, Data Security and personal-information laws gave the CAC its basis to summon Nvidia over H20 "backdoor" risks in July 2025.

本地法律。中国自己的《出口管制法》管的是国家的出口——算力、模型或技术的外流——而非 GPU 进口，因此很少触及入境芯片。真正起作用的是《反外国制裁法》和阻断规则：在华企业若为遵守美国规则而拒绝中国客户，可能被追责——这对任何一边服务中国、一边遵守美国法律的企业都是直接冲突。《网络安全法》《数据安全法》和个人信息保护法，则为网信办约谈英伟达、质疑 H20"后门"风险（2025 年 7 月）提供了依据。

MOFCOM Ann. 91 · 1 Jan 2026 · Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law · 10 Jun 2021 · AP · 31 Jul 2025

Beijing's gate. China publishes no import ban. Approval is informal and case-by-case — by the CAC, MIIT, NDRC and Customs — and steered toward domestic chips. State-funded data centers were told in November 2025 to use domestic AI chips only, and in January 2026 Customs told agents the H200 was "not permitted" to enter except in special cases such as university R&D.

北京的关口。中国没有公布进口禁令。审批是非正式、逐案进行的——由网信办、工信部、发改委和海关掌握——并引导买家转向国产芯片。2025 年 11 月，国家出资的数据中心被要求只用国产 AI 芯片；2026 年 1 月，海关告知代理 H200"不获准"入境，大学研发等特殊情形除外。

Tom's Hardware / Reuters · Nov 2025 press媒体 · Taipei Times · 15 Jan 2026 press媒体

No published rule. Every one of Beijing's import measures is informal — summonses, window guidance, customs practice — not a published regulation. The figures reported here come from press accounts.

没有成文规则。北京的进口措施无一成文——约谈、窗口指导、海关操作——而非公开法规。此处引用的数字来自媒体报道。

Hong Kong香港

For US rules, Hong Kong is China. For its own law, it is not — and that gap is why it matters.

按美国规则，香港就是中国；按香港自己的法律，它不是——这道落差正是关键所在。

Under US law. Since 2020 Hong Kong is treated as part of China. Nvidia and AMD write every China restriction as "China (including Hong Kong and Macau)" — same licenses, same presumptions.

按美国法律。自 2020 年起，香港在出口管制上被视为中国的一部分。英伟达和 AMD 把每项对华限制都写作"中国（含香港、澳门）"——相同的许可证、相同的推定。

NVDA 10-Q · 26 Aug 2026

In the chip trade. Hong Kong is the trade's main re-export point. It handled more than half of China's $239B in chip imports in January–May 2026, and Nvidia warns that its own supply is "warehoused in and distributed from Hong Kong." It is also a named transit point in smuggling cases.

在芯片贸易中。香港是这场贸易的主要再出口点。2026 年 1 至 5 月，中国 2,390 亿美元芯片进口中有一半以上经香港处理；英伟达亦警示其自身供应"存放于香港并由香港分销"。香港也是走私案件中被点名的中转地。

The Next Web · 4 Jul 2026 press媒体 · NVDA 10-Q · 26 Aug 2026

Local law. Hong Kong's own control is the Import and Export Ordinance (Cap. 60). A license is needed to import, export, re-export or transship转运 listed strategic commodities — but the lists follow the multilateral regimes (last updated 30 May 2025), not US-only ECCNs like 3A090. Whether a given GPU server is listed turns on its own classification, such as encryption. Penalty: up to HK$500,000 and 2 years on summary conviction; an unlimited fine and 7 years on indictment, plus forfeiture.

本地法律。香港自己的管制是《进出口条例》（第 60 章）。进口、出口、再出口或转运transship列明战略物品须领牌照——但清单依多边机制（最近一次更新为 2025 年 5 月 30 日），而非 3A090 这类美国专有编码。某款 GPU 服务器是否列管，取决于其自身归类，例如加密。罚则：简易程序定罪最高 50 万港元及 2 年监禁；公诉程序可处无上限罚款及 7 年监禁，并没收货物。

TID circular 5/2025 · 30 May 2025

Local law does not ban these chips. Hong Kong "does not implement, nor do we have the legal authority to take action on, unilateral sanctions imposed by other jurisdictions." The control that reaches these chips is US law — binding any US-origin item and any party subject to the EAR in Hong Kong.

当地法律并不禁止这些芯片。香港"不执行、也无法律权力对其他司法管辖区实施的单边制裁采取行动"。真正管住这些芯片的是美国法律——它约束任何美国原产物项，以及香港境内任何受 EAR 管辖的主体。

HK Gov · 9 Oct 2022

Singapore新加坡

No license for most buyers — but a Singapore billing or transit address does not launder a China-bound chip.

多数买家无需许可——但新加坡的开票或中转地址，并不能"洗白"运往中国的芯片。

Under US law. Singapore is not in Country Group D:1, D:4 or D:5, so 3A090 chips generally ship there without a license — with two exceptions: buyers headquartered in China or Macau (or with a parent there) need a license and face a presumption of denial, and anyone who knows about, or ignores red flags of, diversion to China is caught.

按美国法律。新加坡不属于 D:1、D:4 或 D:5 国家组，因此 3A090 芯片一般无需许可即可运往当地——但有两个例外：总部位于中国或澳门（或母公司在当地）的买家须领许可并面临推定拒绝；明知或无视转运至中国的危险信号者亦被管住。

BIS guidance · 31 May 2026 · 91 FR 1684 · 15 Jan 2026 · BIS diversion guidance · 13 May 2025

Local law. Its own control is the Strategic Goods (Control) Act (section 5): a permit is needed to export, transship, transit or broker listed, Wassenaar-based goods, with a catch-all for WMD end use. Penalty: up to S$100,000 or three times the goods' value, whichever is greater, and up to 2 years; more for repeat offences. Every import, export and transshipment also needs true declarations under the Customs Act — and false declarations are the usual charge in diversion cases.

本地法律。其自身管制是《战略物资（管制）法》（第 5 条）：出口、转运、过境或中介列明的（以《瓦森纳安排》为基础的）物品须领许可，并设有针对大规模杀伤性武器用途的兜底条款。罚则：最高 10 万新元或货值的三倍（取其高者）及最高 2 年监禁；再犯更重。依《海关法》，每一笔进口、出口和转运还须如实申报——转运案件中最常见的指控正是虚假申报。

Singapore Customs · checked 31 Oct 2026

Enforcement. The cases bear this out. Three men were charged in Singapore in 2025 with fraud over servers worth about $390M that may have carried Nvidia chips to China via Malaysia. The Megaspeed investigation — its Malaysian unit bought about $2B of Nvidia chips — is ongoing; it moved from Commerce to the DOJ in 2026, and a former executive faces a US espionage probe (October 2026), still a probe with no charges disclosed. The Earthmade indictment (October 2026) alleges servers routed through Malaysia and Singapore.

执法。案件印证了这一点。2025 年，三名男子在新加坡被控欺诈，涉及约 3.9 亿美元的服务器，其中可能装有经马来西亚运往中国的英伟达芯片。Megaspeed 调查——其马来西亚子公司购入约 20 亿美元英伟达芯片——仍在进行；2026 年由商务部转交司法部，一名前高管面临美国间谍调查（2026 年 10 月），目前仍属调查，尚未公布指控。Earthmade 起诉书（2026 年 10 月）指称服务器经马来西亚和新加坡转运。

CNBC · 10 Oct 2025 press媒体 · Bloomberg · 29 Oct 2026 unconfirmed未确认 · qz.com · checked 31 Oct 2026 press媒体

Charged as fraud, not as an export crime. Singapore's own law does not ban these chips, and it "will not enforce foreign export regimes." The 2025 server case was brought under the Penal Code, and misdeclaring the end user to suppliers is prosecuted as fraud or false declaration — not as a breach of US export law.

以欺诈而非出口犯罪入罪。新加坡自身法律并不禁止这些芯片，而且"不执行外国出口制度"。2025 年服务器案依《刑法典》提控，向供应商虚报最终用户被作为欺诈或虚假申报起诉——而非违反美国出口法。

Dimsum Daily · Oct 2026 press媒体

United Arab Emirates阿联酋

A favored partner since July 2026 — and still not a backdoor to China.

自 2026 年 7 月成为受惠伙伴——但仍不是通往中国的后门。

Under US law. Rule 91 FR 43034 (published 14 Jul 2026, effective 10 Jul 2026) moved the UAE out of Country Groups D:3/D:4 into A:5. G42 and Core42 may receive advanced computing items license-free in the UAE — a status that lapses on 6 April 2027 unless they become US companies. Amazon, Apple, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle and X.AI are license-free and STA-eligible; MGX gets a favorable review. Everyone else still needs a license for 3A090, and exports to parties headquartered in China (D:5) or Macau still need a license. Earlier, in November 2025, Commerce had authorized up to 35,000 GB300-equivalents each for G42 and Humain, conditioned on security and reporting requirements.

按美国法律。91 FR 43034 号规则（2026 年 7 月 14 日公布，7 月 10 日生效）将阿联酋移出 D:3/D:4 国家组，纳入 A:5。G42 和 Core42 可在阿联酋免许可接收先进计算物项——该身份将于 2027 年 4 月 6 日失效，除非两家成为美国公司。亚马逊、苹果、谷歌、Meta、微软、OpenAI、甲骨文和 X.AI 免许可并符合 STA 资格；MGX 享优惠审查。其余各方采购 3A090 仍需许可，向总部位于中国（D:5）或澳门的主体出口亦仍需许可。此前的 2025 年 11 月，商务部曾批准 G42 与 Humain 各获至多 35,000 个 GB300 等效算力，并附安全与报告条件。

91 FR 43034 · 14 Jul 2026 · Covington · Jul 2026 · Commerce · Nov 2025

In the chip trade. The UAE is the Gulf's compute hub: the favorable treatment is meant to build AI data centers there under US oversight, not to open a path onward to China.

在芯片贸易中。阿联酋是海湾地区的算力枢纽：这套优惠待遇旨在美国监督下于当地建设 AI 数据中心，而非开辟通往中国的后续路径。

Local law. The UAE's own control is Federal Decree-Law No. 43 of 2021 on non-proliferation goods, with Cabinet Decision 97/2024, run by the Executive Office for Control & Non-Proliferation. It licenses the export, re-export and transit of listed dual-use goods, with a list that mirrors the multilateral regimes — but no AI-chip-specific UAE listing has been found.

本地法律。阿联酋自身管制是 2021 年第 43 号关于防扩散物品的联邦法令，配合 2024 年第 97 号内阁决定，由管制与防扩散执行办公室执行。它对列明两用物品的出口、再出口和过境发放许可，清单与多边机制一致——但尚未发现针对 AI 芯片的阿联酋专项列管。

Morgan Lewis · Aug 2025

Not a backdoor to China. Favorable treatment or not, exports to parties headquartered in China or Macau still need a US license. The UAE's own law does not ban these chips — the control is US law — and the whole arrangement rests on anti-diversion commitments.

不是通往中国的后门。无论是否享受优惠待遇，向总部位于中国或澳门的主体出口仍需美国许可证。阿联酋自身法律并不禁止这些芯片——管住它们的是美国法律——而整套安排都建立在反转运承诺之上。

91 FR 43034 · 14 Jul 2026

One gap. The rule text does not address remote access by Chinese users, so a licensed party's own conditions govern that — and those conditions are not public. G42 is reportedly exploring US majority ownership to keep its access past April 2027.

一处空白。规则文本未涉及中国用户的远程访问，因此由获许可方自身的条件约束——而这些条件并不公开。据报道，G42 正在探索让美国投资者取得多数股权，以保住 2027 年 4 月后的准入。
